Brandon Quach

I am a senior at Yale studying Molecular Biophysics & Biochemistry with a minor in Data Science. My academic work focuses on using machine learning methods to design novel enhancers for cell-type-specific experiments in mosquito brains. I am most interested in grounding computational methods in in vivo experiments, drawing on concepts like "lab in the loop" and papers such as DeepLIFT.

Outside of the lab, I think a lot about how to brew the perfect cup of tea. In science education, I re-founded The Green Cafe to teach biology at the Marsh Botanical Garden, serve as a Senior Staff Writer for the Yale Scientific Magazine, and co-direct the Science and Civics Fellowship with CU Society.
